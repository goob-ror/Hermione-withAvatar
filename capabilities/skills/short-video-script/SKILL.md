# Short-video script

1. **Research the angle** if needed: `video_search` for what's working on the topic; `video_inspect` a top video for its hook and structure. Don't copy — find the angle.
2. **Hook (0–3 s):** one line that creates curiosity, tension or a promise ("Most people waste money on X — here's the fix."). Concrete beats vague.
3. **Beats:** 4–8 beats, each one sentence of voiceover (≈ 2–4 s), with:
   - `on_screen`: 2–5 punchy words,
   - `broll`: a concrete visual search phrase (for Pexels).
4. **Payoff + call to action** in the last beat (save, follow, comment a word).
5. Language: match the audience (Indonesian scripts for Indonesian audiences). Short words, no filler.
6. Ready to render? Hand the beats to `clip_render`.
